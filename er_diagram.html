BusinessEntities Hierarchy (Disjoint 'd')

  • entityId (Primary Key) PK
  • Suppliers (supplierId) ISA Subclass
  • Manufacturers (manufId) ISA Subclass
  • Customers (customerId) ISA Subclass
  • Shippers (shipperId) ISA Subclass

Items Hierarchy (Overlapping 'o')

  • itemId (Primary Key) PK
  • unitWeight (Positive NUMBER) > 0
  • MaterialItems (Component parts) ISA Subclass
  • ProductItems (Assembled goods) ISA Subclass
  • BillOfMaterials (BOM Association) M:N BOM

Key Business Constraints

  • No Self-Shipment sender <> recipient
  • Non-Reflexive BOM prodItem <> matItem
  • Tiered Discounts amt2 >= amt1
  • Marginal Rate Check disc2 >= disc1
  • Positive Quantities qty > 0
